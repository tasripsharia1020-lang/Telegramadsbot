About ANV VERSE
ANV VERSE is a community-powered digital rewards platform. Members can earn ANV through mining cycles, daily check-ins, verified community tasks, eligible rewarded ads and referrals.

Rewards are recorded by the server. ANV is an in-app reward point and its indicative planning rate is not a guaranteed market price.

USDT withdrawals are submitted from the separate USDT app balance and are reviewed by the admin. A request is not a completed payment until the transaction is sent and marked Paid.

ANV-to-USDT swap is not enabled yet. Follow official announcements for updates.
