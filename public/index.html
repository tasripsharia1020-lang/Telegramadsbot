About ANV VERSE
ANV VERSE is a community-powered digital rewards platform where members can mine ANV, complete daily and social tasks, watch eligible rewarded ads, and invite friends to grow their rewards. Our goal is to make earning simple, transparent, and enjoyable through a clean experience built around the ANV ecosystem. Members can collect ANV through a 24-hour mining cycle, daily check-ins, verified tasks, and eligible referrals. Reward credits are recorded by our secure server to help keep the system fair and prevent duplicate claims. ANV withdrawal requests are available in USDT on the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20), subject to the minimum amount, account checks, available payout funds, and admin review. A submitted request is not a completed payment; the status will update after the admin processes it. ANV-to-USDT swap is not enabled yet. It will be opened only after the conversion reserve, exchange rules, and settlement process are ready. Follow official ANV VERSE announcements for updates, and contact @Vertusq if you need help.
Need help? Contact our Telegram support team at @Vertusq. Your Telegram ID will be included in the message.
